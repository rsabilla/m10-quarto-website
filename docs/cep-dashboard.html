What the acquisition data says
  • Instagram drives over half of all traffic (472 of 902 sessions, 52%) with a strong 76% engagement rate, but visits are short (26s).
  • Google organic visitors stay twice as long (56s vs. 26s), so search brings people who are actively looking for services.
  • Direct traffic has the lowest engagement (57%), likely people checking the homepage quickly.
  • Key events = 0 in reports so far. generate_lead was only just confirmed in DebugView, so lead counts should start appearing in the next reporting window.

What the content data says
  • The homepage gets 50% of all views but holds attention for only 12s, so it’s the biggest opportunity for the impact-stats redesign the client asked for.
  • Volunteer (52s) and Therapy (50s) pages hold attention the longest, which shows strong intent on the pages tied to the client’s goals.
  • Contact (gold) gets 84 views but only 18s of engagement. That’s fine for a form page, but it’s worth tracking how many of those turn into generate_lead.
  • 88% of users are on mobile, so every redesign and ad landing page should be built mobile-first.
