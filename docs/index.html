Hi, I’m Ron! I’m a graduate student in the Master of Science in Digital Marketing (MSDM) program at Cal Poly Pomona. I like working where data and storytelling meet: figuring out what the numbers say, then turning that into content people actually want to watch or read.

Right now I’m on an MSDM capstone team helping a real nonprofit client, Wellness Ranch Therapy, use their website data to reach more people. I also create video and promo content with Associated Students, Inc. (ASI) at Cal Poly Pomona.

Current Capstone: Wellness Ranch Therapy

Wellness Ranch Therapy is a nonprofit in Rancho Cucamonga that offers equine-assisted therapy. Our team is helping them grow their reach and donations through website & SEO improvements, GA4 analytics, and Google Ad Grants. Here’s what I’m working on:

📊 GA4 tracking validation

Verified the site’s events in GA4 DebugView, including page_view, scroll, form_start, and the generate_lead key event that fires when someone submits the Contact form.

🔎 Baseline traffic analysis

In the first weeks of tracking, the site had 902 sessions from 764 users. 52% came from Instagram, and 88% were on mobile.

📄 Content insights

The homepage gets half of all views but only 12 seconds of engagement, while the Volunteer and Therapy pages hold visitors the longest.

🎯 Google Ad Grants (AO4)

After launch, I’ll analyze the Ad Grants campaign to improve click-through and conversion rates while keeping the grant compliant.
