Objective

This page analyzes traffic to my own portfolio website. The goal is to understand who visits, how they find the site, and whether they reach the pages that matter most to a recruiter: my projects and my resume.

Research questions

  1. Which channels bring visitors to the site (organic search, direct, referral, social)?
  2. Which search queries lead people to the site in Google?
  3. Which pages hold attention, and which lose visitors quickly?
  4. How many visitors reach a “conversion” page, such as the Resume or a project case study?

BigQuery Analysis

Example query used to count sessions by traffic source from the GA4 export:

sessions_by_source.sql
SELECT
  traffic_source.source AS source,
  traffic_source.medium AS medium,
  COUNT(DISTINCT CONCAT(user_pseudo_id,
        CAST((SELECT value.int_value FROM UNNEST(event_params)
              WHERE key = 'ga_session_id') AS STRING))) AS sessions
FROM `YOUR-PROJECT.analytics_YOUR-PROPERTY-ID.events_*`
WHERE _TABLE_SUFFIX BETWEEN '20261101' AND '20261130'
GROUP BY source, medium
ORDER BY sessions DESC;
